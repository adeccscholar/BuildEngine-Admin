## When compilation is not enough

Boost 1.92 integration exposed a more difficult issue than a syntax error: the **C++ stream and serialization boundary across DLLs**. The development history contains separate diagnostics for locale and codecvt, text archives, serialization linkage, uncaught exceptions, and `std::ostream` crossing a DLL boundary. These are ABI and runtime-consumer questions that a successful static compile cannot answer.

The record includes an explicit decision to **accept a known BCC64X text-archive boundary limitation** rather than hide it. A properly constrained limitation is better evidence than a fabricated global success claim.

## Why this shaped BuildEngine

The incident reinforced the need for isolated smoke programs, controlled DLL runtime settings, and test evidence that belongs to a particular build variant and exact installed binary. It also influenced the later preference for a mandatory dynamic BCC64X runtime and carefully documented import-library contracts.
